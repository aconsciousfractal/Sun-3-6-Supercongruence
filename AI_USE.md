# AI assistance and responsibility

AI assistance under the author's direction was used for mathematical
exploration, proof criticism, source comparison, manuscript drafting,
exact computation and repository preparation. OpenAI Codex and GPT-6.1 Sol
assisted with consolidation and separate checks of the proof and companion.
Research supplied through an external agent was also examined; its complete
model provenance is not asserted.

The systems had access to related arguments. Their checks are not independent
human peer review, formal proof verification or a determination of priority.
Individual proof steps are not attributed to particular AI systems.
Oleksiy Babanskyy is the human author and is responsible for the manuscript,
source attribution, licence boundaries and final decision to publish.
